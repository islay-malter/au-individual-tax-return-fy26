## Source-Use Rules

- Record page title, exact URL, applicable year, status, and access date in the working assessment.
- Quote only short excerpts; prefer paraphrase and links.
- Separate enacted FY26 rules from substantive future-year rules and unresolved proposals.
- Treat post-year-end lodgment/reporting changes that operate for the FY26 return separately from substantive rules that first apply to the 2026-27 income year.
- If pre-fill disagrees with the issuer document, record both and the resolution path.
- `B1`, `U2`, and `H1` are FY26 trust statement-of-distribution labels that support pre-fill and IT5; they are not new individual paper-return question numbers.
- Ceasing employment is not, by itself, a deferred ESS taxing point. Reconcile the actual plan facts and applicable taxing-point rule.
- Retain cost-base and unapplied-loss records for as long as they continue to support later calculations, even where ordinary return-record periods would otherwise have passed.
