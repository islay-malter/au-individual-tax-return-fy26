# FY26 Evidence Checklists

Keep evidence already available separate from evidence still to obtain. Unknown intake facts become questions; they do not make every category mandatory.

## Core Evidence

- Official tax-agent/ATO pre-filling report or myTax pre-filled data for 2025-26. Label any saved myTax draft/customer copy accurately.
- Prior-year lodged return and Notice of Assessment, if obtainable.
- Valid Australian refund-account details available to provide directly to the lodger; do not store the account identifiers in the profile or working assessment.
- Source documents for each income, withholding, offset, rebate, and deduction included.

## Employment And Payments

- Tax-ready income statement/payment summary for each employer.
- Final payslip where useful for payroll, allowance, RFBA, reportable-super, or 12% super-guarantee reconciliation.
- Statements for termination payments, government payments, paid parental leave, workers compensation, and salary packaging where applicable.

## Household, Medicare, And Health

- Full spouse income-test components; dependant DOB/student/adjusted-income/maintenance facts and relevant status dates.
- Private health statement or pre-fill detail for each policy, including insured-person ages, hospital-versus-extras status, covered days, benefit codes, premiums/rebate shares, and policy/family-status changes.
- Medicare Entitlement Statement or other exemption evidence when relevant.

## Interest, Shares, ETFs, Trusts, And CGT

- Bank interest summaries for all accounts, including joint ownership proportions.
- Broker EOFY tax report and CSV export; buy/sell contract notes for disposals.
- Dividend statements and registry transaction history, including DRP events.
- ETF, managed-fund, AMIT, and trust annual tax statements; AMIT cost-base adjustments.
- Corporate-action records: splits, consolidations, demergers, returns of capital, takeovers, and reorganisations.
- Prior-year carried-forward capital-loss records and an auditable CGT report, if available.
- CGT schedule working papers where current-year capital gains exceed $10,000.
- Foreign income and tax-withheld statements plus the FX source/method.

Do not rely on pre-filled trust-distribution data or a single broker summary without reconciling it to the issuer's annual statement and underlying transactions.

## ESS And Employee Equity

- ESS statement from each employer, or written foreign-employer details, plus award/plan rules and startup-concession status.
- Grant, associate acquisition, vest, exercise, cessation, restructure/corporate action, sale, and year-end holding records.
- Market value, amount paid, discount, taxing-point basis, sale proceeds, tax withheld, and FX records.
- Prior deferred-ESS records, overseas-workday evidence, and a cost-base bridge from the ESS taxing point to later CGT.

## Work-Related Deductions

- Full-period work-from-home hours records and at least one record for every covered additional running-expense category actually incurred.
- Receipts and work-use basis for equipment, software, subscriptions, phone/internet, training, memberships, tools, clothing, travel, and other claims.
- Car logbook/odometer and cost records for the logbook method, or work-kilometre records for the cents-per-kilometre method.
- Employer reimbursements and allowances.

## Super And Study Loans

- Super annual statement and contribution history where material.
- Personal-contribution receipt and acknowledged notice of intent before claiming a deduction.
- Division 293 or excess-contribution determinations.
- Current study/training-loan account details where a working estimate must reflect the FY26 marginal compulsory-repayment system and any 20% balance reduction.

## Other Conditional Evidence

- PAYG instalment notices and account/payment evidence.
- Rental ownership/acquisition/disposal records; leases/availability listings; private-use calendar; floor-area basis; loan/refinance tracing; repairs-versus-improvements evidence; capital works/depreciation; platform statements; retained-bond/insurance records; council, water, agent, and body-corporate records.
- Sole-trader/business/PSI ABN/status and accounting basis; income/expense records; TPAR/grants; opening/closing stock; bank/invoice/BAS/GST records; asset register; non-commercial-loss evidence; and prior schedules.
- Partnership/trust statements and distribution advice, reconciled to any new FY26 pre-fill detail.
- Foreign-income records split by employment, pension, rental, investment, or entity/attributed-income type, with country, gross amount, foreign tax/FITO evidence, FX method/date, allocation facts, foreign return, and aggregate foreign-asset value at any time.
- Crypto exchange CSVs, wallet exports, and completeness checks covering AUD values/valuation source, fees, same-owner wallet mapping, swaps, gifts, staking/rewards, NFTs, DeFi, wrapping/bridges, and liquidity-pool events.
- Gift/contribution receipts and classification evidence. Use official DGR lookup evidence for ordinary gifts to DGRs; retain the distinct evidence required for political contributions, property gifts, or contributions involving a material benefit.
- ATO general/shortfall interest transaction details split by incurred date; charges incurred on or after 1 July 2025 are not deductible.
- Income-protection premium statement, foreign-income records, and other source documents triggered by intake.

For complex residency, ESS, foreign tax, crypto, trust, rental/business, deceased-estate, or unusual CGT matters, the checklist supports evidence collection but does not remove the need for registered-tax-agent review.
