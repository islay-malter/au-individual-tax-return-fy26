## FY26 Tax Calculation And Scenarios

Unless a separate deterministic tool is explicitly used and named, state that the calculation is model-produced. Show inputs, formulas, intermediate components, and the final reconciliation. Keep income tax, Medicare levy/reduction, MLS, private-health rebate adjustment, study/training-loan repayment, offsets/credits, PAYG withholding, and PAYG instalments separate.

## Independent Arithmetic Check

Record the independent method, myTax comparison, or registered-tax-agent review; its date; material differences; and their resolution. Until this exists, keep the arithmetic status `Unchecked model-produced working scenario`.

## FY26 Change Validation

Confirm the HELP marginal formula, split-period PHI rates, FY26 Medicare low-income thresholds, 70c WFH rate, 88c car rate, and exclusion of later/proposed settings. Record any GIC/SIC adjustment by incurred date.

## Accountant Email Content

When the user asks for an email, state that the return is for the year ended 30 June 2026, list the attached evidence, and group questions by materiality. Do not include TFNs, credentials, or full bank identifiers in the email draft.
