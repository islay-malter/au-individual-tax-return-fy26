# FY26 Rates And Thresholds

Applies only to the Australian `2025-26` income year, 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026. The initial source set was verified on `2026-08-30`; study-loan, GDP-adjustment, Lump Sum E, and instant-asset-write-off settings were independently reverified on `2026-08-31`.

Re-open the linked official source before a material calculation. If an official page conflicts with this snapshot, record the discrepancy and use the enacted substantive rule that applies to FY26. Exclude substantive rules that first apply to a later income year, but retain post-year-end lodgment or reporting changes that operate for the FY26 return.

## Individual Income-Tax Rates

### Australian residents

| Taxable income | FY26 income tax |
|---:|---:|
| $0-$18,200 | Nil |
| $18,201-$45,000 | 16c for each $1 over $18,200 |
| $45,001-$135,000 | $4,288 plus 30c for each $1 over $45,000 |
| $135,001-$190,000 | $31,288 plus 37c for each $1 over $135,000 |
| $190,001 and over | $51,638 plus 45c for each $1 over $190,000 |

Medicare levy is additional. Source: [ATO - Tax rates: Australian residents](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/tax-rates-australian-residents).

### Foreign residents

| Taxable income | FY26 income tax |
|---:|---:|
| $0-$135,000 | 30c for each $1 |
| $135,001-$190,000 | $40,500 plus 37c for each $1 over $135,000 |
| $190,001 and over | $60,850 plus 45c for each $1 over $190,000 |

There is no tax-free threshold. A full-year foreign resident generally does not pay Medicare levy; part-year and temporary-resident cases require date- and fact-specific treatment. Source: [ATO - Tax rates: Foreign residents](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/tax-rates-foreign-residents).

Do not apply either table mechanically to part-year residents, temporary residents, working holiday makers, minors, deceased estates, or special professional-income averaging cases.

## Common Tax Offsets

### Low income tax offset (LITO)

Entitlement requires the individual to be an Australian resident at some time during the income year; special rules can apply to minors.

| Taxable income | FY26 LITO |
|---:|---:|
| $0-$37,500 | $700 |
| $37,501-$45,000 | $700 less 5c per $1 over $37,500 |
| $45,001-$66,667 | $325 less 1.5c per $1 over $45,000 |
| Above $66,667 | Nil |

The low and middle income tax offset ended after 2021-22; do not include it.

### Seniors and pensioners tax offset (SAPTO)

| Status | Maximum | Shade-out threshold | Cut-out threshold |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Single | $2,230 | $34,919 | $52,759 |
| Each member of a couple | $1,602 | $30,994 | $43,810 |
| Each member of an illness-separated couple | $2,040 | $33,732 | $50,052 |

The offset reduces by 12.5c per $1 above the shade-out threshold. Eligibility and couple-combined rules must be checked before use.

Other offsets, including FITO, spouse-super, small-business-income, invalid/carer, beneficiary, and lump-sum-in-arrears offsets, are intake-triggered. Do not manufacture a general offsets total.

## Medicare Levy

The general Medicare levy rate is 2%.

FY26 low-income settings enacted for assessments for 2025-26 include:

| Category | No-levy threshold | Further setting |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ordinary individual | $28,011 | 10% shade-in; phase-in limit $35,013 |
| SAPTO-eligible individual | $44,268 | Phase-in limit $55,335 |
| Family | $47,238 | Add $4,338 for each dependent child/student |
| SAPTO-eligible family | $61,623 | Add $4,338 for each dependent child/student |

Family reduction, exemption days, entitlement gaps, spouse status, and part-year residency require fact-specific calculations. Source: [Medicare Levy Act 1986, compilation effective 1 July 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03351/2026-07-01/text).

## Medicare Levy Surcharge

| Tier | Single income for MLS purposes | Family income for MLS purposes | Rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Base | $101,000 or less | $202,000 or less | 0% |
| Tier 1 | $101,001-$118,000 | $202,001-$236,000 | 1% |
| Tier 2 | $118,001-$158,000 | $236,001-$316,000 | 1.25% |
| Tier 3 | $158,001 and over | $316,001 and over | 1.5% |

Increase the family threshold by $1,500 for each dependent child after the first. Test income, family status, and appropriate hospital cover by the relevant days; extras-only cover is not sufficient. Source: [ATO - Medicare levy surcharge income, thresholds and rates](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/medicare-and-private-health-insurance/medicare-levy-surcharge/medicare-levy-surcharge-income-thresholds-and-rates).

## Study And Training Support Loans

FY26 uses a new marginal compulsory-repayment system:

| Repayment income | Compulsory repayment before the remaining-debt cap |
|---:|---:|
| $67,000 or less | Nil |
| $67,001-$125,000 | 15% of repayment income above $67,000 |
| $125,001-$179,285 | $8,700 plus 17% of repayment income above $125,000 |
| $179,286 and over | 10% of total repayment income |

The statutory result is the least of the marginal amount, 10% of total repayment income, and the person's repayable debt. The published bands reflect where the marginal amount or 10% cap controls; never calculate more than the remaining debt. A separate Medicare-levy exception in the governing provisions can make the liability nil in some low-income cases.

Repayment income includes taxable income plus total net investment loss, reportable fringe benefits, reportable super contributions, and exempt foreign employment income. One hierarchy covers HELP, VSL, SFSS, SSL, ABSTUDY SSL, and AASL.

The legislated 20% qualifying-debt reduction is an ATO loan-account adjustment, not an income-tax deduction or offset. Use the actual ATO loan account balance. PAYG study-loan withholding changed from 24 September 2025, so withholding earlier in FY26 may legitimately follow the old settings. Sources: [ATO - Study and training loan rates and repayment thresholds](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/study-and-training-support-loans-rates-and-repayment-thresholds), [Higher Education Support Act 2003, sections 154-1 to 154-20](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A01234/latest/text), and [ATO - Study and training loans: What's new](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/study-and-training-support-loans/study-and-training-loans-what-s-new).

## Super And Division 293

- Super guarantee rate: 12%.
- Maximum SG contribution base: $62,500 per quarter; maximum statutory SG $7,500 per quarter.
- Concessional contributions cap: $30,000.
- Non-concessional contributions cap: $120,000.
- FY26 bring-forward availability, subject to age and prior bring-forward status: total super balance below $1.76 million may allow up to $360,000 over three years; $1.76 million to below $1.88 million may allow $240,000 over two years; $1.88 million to below $2 million permits only the annual $120,000 cap; $2 million or more gives a nil non-concessional cap.
- General transfer balance cap from 1 July 2025: $2 million.
- Carry-forward concessional amounts may be available from the prior five years if the previous 30 June total super balance was below $500,000.
- Division 293 threshold: $250,000; additional tax is 15% of the lesser of Division 293 contributions and the excess over the threshold.

Division 293 is ordinarily assessed after fund reporting and may not appear on the ordinary income-tax notice of assessment. Sources: [ATO - Key super rates and thresholds](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds) and [ATO - Division 293 tax](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/super-for-individuals-and-families/super/growing-and-keeping-track-of-your-super/caps-limits-and-tax-on-super-contributions/division-293-tax-on-concessional-contributions-by-high-income-earners).

## Work-Related Methods

- Working-from-home fixed rate: 70c per hour. Keep a contemporaneous record of all hours and at least one record for every covered expense category actually incurred. The rate covers energy, phone and internet usage, stationery, and computer consumables; do not double claim them. Eligible decline in value, repairs/maintenance, and dedicated-office cleaning may remain separate.
- Cents-per-kilometre car rate: 88c per eligible kilometre, capped at 5,000 work/business kilometres per car. Keep a reasonable basis for the kilometres.

Sources: [ATO - Fixed rate method](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/income-deductions-offsets-and-records/deductions-you-can-claim/work-related-deductions/working-from-home-expenses/fixed-rate-method) and [2024 cents-per-kilometre determination](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2024L00697/asmade).

## Other Confirmed FY26 Changes

- General interest charge and shortfall interest charge incurred on or after 1 July 2025 are not deductible. Separate charges by incurred date.
- Eligible DGR gifts made from 1 July 2024 no longer have a $2 minimum, but DGR, receipt, benefit, property, and political-gift rules still apply.
- Trust distributions may be pre-filled from lodged trust returns. Reconcile to trustee statements; `B1`, `U2`, and `H1` are trust statement-of-distribution labels, not new individual paper-return questions.
- From 1 July 2025, Schedule 5 removed the former $1,200 Lump Sum E reporting threshold. Any qualifying back payment of remuneration accrued or payable more than 12 months before payment must be reported as Lump Sum E regardless of amount. This does not remove the separate eligibility tests for the lump-sum-in-arrears tax offset or Medicare levy adjustment. Sources: [Taxation Administration (Withholding Schedules) Instrument 2025, Schedule 5](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L00647/asmade) and [ATO - 2025 PAYG withholding tax tables](https://softwaredevelopers.ato.gov.au/2025-pay-you-go-payg-withholding-tax-tables).
- For the 2025-26 income year, the GDP adjustment factor is 4% for Commissioner-calculated quarterly GST and PAYG instalment amounts. For PAYG, this is the instalment amount method, not the rate method or PAYG withholding; for GST, it is the GST instalments method, not ordinary quarterly actual-GST reporting. Check substituted accounting periods: income years beginning on 1 January, 1 February, or 1 March 2025 retain the 2024-25 factor of 6%, while income years beginning from 1 April 2025 use 4%. Source: [ATO - GDP adjustment for 2025-26 GST and PAYG instalments](https://softwaredevelopers.ato.gov.au/gdp-adjustment-2025-26-gst-and-payg-instalments).
- Small businesses with aggregated turnover under $10 million may use a $20,000 instant asset write-off per asset first used or installed ready for use by 30 June 2026, subject to the simplified-depreciation conditions. Source: [Treasury Laws Amendment (Strengthening Financial Systems and Other Measures) Act 2025, Schedule 7](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2025A00072/latest).
- Plug-in hybrid EVs generally ceased qualifying for the electric-car FBT exemption from 1 April 2025 unless protected by the specific transitional conditions; this may affect RFBA and downstream income tests. Source: [ATO - Electric cars exemption](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/hiring-and-paying-your-workers/fringe-benefits-tax/types-of-fringe-benefits/fbt-on-cars-other-vehicles-parking-and-tolls/electric-cars-exemption).

## Do Not Apply To FY26

- The $1,000 standard work-expense deduction is enacted for 2026-27, and the Working Australians Tax Offset and CGT/negative-gearing reforms are enacted for 2027-28; none applies to FY26.
- The resident 16% bracket falls to 15% only from 1 July 2026, for the 2026-27 income year.
- The car rate becomes 91c only from 1 July 2026.
- Other announced or proposed private-health and later-year measures do not apply unless enacted with explicit FY26 effect.

Source for the enacted future measures: [Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2026A00049/latest).
